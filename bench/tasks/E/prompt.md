Now a "Today" panel at the top of the trades page, above the blotter. It's called "Today" (that's what screen readers should hear for it too) and it shows a row of figures for today's trades: "Trades" with the total, then "Filled", "Partially filled", "Cancelled" and "Pending" each with how many of today's trades have that status. Each one a label with its number next to it, like the stats in the trade drawer. Important: "today" is the trading day, and our trading day is the UTC day, midnight to midnight UTC, full stop. We have people in Tokyo and New York looking at this, and a trade at 23:59 UTC is still that day's trade for all of them, and one at 00:01 UTC is the next day's, whatever their laptop clock says. All the times we show are UTC already so this is just the same rule.

Okay, the big ones.

One: booking trades. Right now Pulse only shows trades, the desk wants to book them from here. A "Book trade" button in the blotter header next to Export CSV. It opens a dialog called "Book trade" with:
- "Client", a dropdown of client names, A to Z
- "Instrument", a dropdown with the symbol then the name for each, like "AAPL Apple Inc."
- "Side", a dropdown with "Buy" and "Sell"
- "Quantity", a whole number
- "Price", which starts at the instrument's last price and follows when you change the instrument, but you can type over it
- and a "Book" button and a "Cancel" button.
If the quantity is empty, zero, negative or not a whole number say "Enter a quantity above zero", and if the price is empty or zero say "Enter a price above zero", and don't book anything. When it books, the dialog closes and the page says "Trade T-10151 booked." (with the real id). The new trade gets the next trade id after the highest one we have, so the first one booked is T-10151, it's executed now, the trader is "D. Whitaker" since that's who's logged in, the venue is "Pulse", fees are 0, and it's a new status, "Pending": nothing is filled yet, it's an order we've sent and not heard back on. It should show up straight away everywhere trades show, the blotter (at the top, it's the newest), the Today panel, the status filter, the drawer, the CSV, all of it, no reload. It's fine if booked trades are gone after a reload, the real backend isn't wired up yet, this is all the mock API. And this dialog has to work properly from the keyboard, a couple of the desk only use the keyboard: when it opens you should be in it, Escape closes it, and when it closes you're back on the Book trade button.

The rule for Pending: a pending trade can be cancelled, nothing else can. So in the trade drawer, a "Cancel trade" button, but only on pending trades. It turns the trade into Cancelled, and the drawer and the blotter both show that right away. Filled and partially filled trades never get the button, those are done and need a call to the broker.

Two: alerts on big bookings. In Settings, in the Alerts panel, add a number box "Large trade alert (USD)", starting at 1,000,000, saved with the other settings. When someone books a trade whose value in USD (quantity times price, and for the EUR and JPY instruments convert it with the same FX rates the portfolio uses) is at or above that, put an unread notification in the bell, titled like "Large trade booked: T-10151", so the count goes up by one. Something short underneath like "Buy 10,000 AAPL for Harbor Pension Trust" is fine. Clicking it should take you to that trade with the drawer open (you've got the links from above for that). Under the threshold, nothing happens.

Three: client exposure. New page "Exposure" in the sidebar, right after Trades, at /exposure, and the top bar says "Exposure". There's a "Client" dropdown with the client names A to Z, and before you pick one it just says "Pick a client to see their exposure." Once you pick one there's a heading like "Exposure for Harbor Pension Trust" and a table called "Exposure by instrument" with one row per instrument the client has traded, A to Z, and the columns "Instrument", "Bought", "Sold", "Net" and "Trades". Bought and Sold add up the filled quantity of their buys and sells, Net is bought minus sold with a minus sign when they're net short (Harbor in AAPL is 0 bought, 3,950 sold, so -3,950), and Trades is how many orders they have in that instrument whatever the status, cancelled and pending included. Quantities formatted with commas like everywhere else. Put the client in the address, /exposure?client=c-002, so I can link to it, and in the client drawer on the Clients page add a link "View exposure" that goes to this page for that client.

Two things on exposure from experience. We had something like this in the old tool and the thing everyone hated was that if you clicked through clients quickly it would sometimes show you one client's positions under another client's name, which is how somebody nearly rang Harbor about Sable's book. Whatever you do, the name on the screen and the numbers under it have to belong to the same client, and it has to be the one you picked last. And second, loading a client takes a moment every time, so once a client's exposure has loaded, keep it so flipping back to that client is instant. Also the trades endpoint is the expensive one, the API team keeps reminding us not to call it more than we need to, so be sensible. I don't mind how you get the trades for a client, a separate call for one client's trades is fine if that's cleaner, as long as what's on screen is right.
